About the Network

The North American Weather Radio Network delivers continuous, real-time weather and hazard information for communities throughout the NAFA coverage area, including the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

Station Details

WXL20 operates on 162.550 MHz and is dedicated to emergency weather information, short-term forecasts, and public safety advisories for the Rochester area and neighboring regions.

Why It Matters

Weather radio is one of the most reliable tools for receiving severe weather warnings and emergency information when other forms of communications may be disrupted.
